C. CONCLUSIONS:
   1. Is Jittering genuinely better here? Still ranks #1 after correction on the quality framework (corrected combined 0.824), BUT flagged HIGH memorisation risk (near-duplicate rate 1.000 vs ~0.101 baseline): it largely RESAMPLES real data. Treat it as a strong augmentation baseline, not a novel generator.
   2. Was Jittering artificially advantaged? Yes, partly: its memorisation rate (1.000) shows it sits on real samples, which inflated coverage/density/nn in the original (leak removed by the held-out reference + novelty score).
   3. Are VAE/TimeGAN evaluated fairly? Yes — identical held-out reference, identical scaler/embedding, identical metrics and seeds for all three; their poor scores reflect genuinely poor distributions (Hellinger near 1.0 on this small, high-dim data), not an implementation penalty.
   4. Which to select for the classifier stage: Jitter_X_imu on the corrected combined score (but see its HIGH memorisation flag above — the downstream check II.8 on unseen real data is the decisive test before committing to it).
   5. Best sensor combination: IMU+Radar (from the quality-based sensor analysis).
   6. Limitations: real N~99 with up to 1905 features makes every metric approximate; the held-out reference removes the trivial leak but full de-leaking needs jitter regenerated from the gen split; Hellinger uses a Gaussian approximation; downstream utility (II.8) is the strongest evidence and needs labels.
